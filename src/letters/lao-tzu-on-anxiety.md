Friend,

You ask how to calm your mind. You have tried breathing apps, lists, long talks with yourself at night. The more you try to make the worry stop, the louder it grows.

Of course it does.

Have you ever stirred a pond to make it clear? The harder you stir, the more mud rises. Pull the mud out with your hands, and the water only clouds again. There is one way to clear muddy water. Leave it alone. Let it be still. Slowly, by itself, the mud sinks. The water becomes clear without anyone making it so.

Your mind is like that water. Anxiety is the mud. Every effort to fight it is more stirring.

This is hard to hear in a world that praises effort. You were taught that a problem is solved by pushing. Some problems are. But the restless mind is not solved by pushing. It is quieted by not pushing. By sitting. By waiting. By letting the mud do what mud does.

So do less.

Not nothing. You still eat, work, care for the people near you. But stop doing extra. Stop checking the thing you already checked. Stop running the same conversation in your head for the tenth time. Stop trying to know tomorrow. When you notice yourself stirring, take your hand out of the water.

Watch the river. It does not hurry. It does not argue with the rocks. It goes around them, under them, over them. It is the softest thing there is, and in time it wears away stone. The worried mind thinks strength means holding on hard. But what is rigid breaks. What is soft and yielding lasts.

Look at how you spend a day. Rushing from one thing to the next. Reading the news, then the messages, then the news again. Filling every quiet minute with a screen. Then you wonder why the mind will not rest. A cup that is always being filled cannot be still. Leave some minutes empty. Walk without listening to anything. Wait in a queue without looking at your phone. At first the emptiness will feel uncomfortable, even frightening. Stay with it. The discomfort is the mud beginning to sink.

Be kind to the part of you that worries. It is trying to keep you safe, the way a nervous dog barks at every sound in the night. Shouting at the dog only makes it bark louder. Sit beside it. Let it see that nothing is coming through the door. Slowly, it lies down.

Notice, too, that the worry is not you. It moves through you, the way clouds move through the sky. The sky does not need to chase the clouds away. It simply stays sky. Clouds come, clouds go. You do not have to believe every cloud.

A small practice. Once a day, sit for the time it takes to drink a cup of tea. Do not try to empty your mind. Just watch what comes. Thoughts will rise. Let them rise. Let them sink. Sip the tea. When it is finished, stand up and go on with your day. That is all. You are not trying to win anything. You are only letting the water settle a little.

Go outside when you can. Trees, wind, a patch of sky between buildings. Nature is not anxious and does not rush, yet everything gets done. Spend time near that kind of patience. Some of it will rub off.

And if the mud is so thick that you cannot see at all, if days go by and nothing settles, ask for help from someone skilled in such things. A good doctor, a good counsellor. Even the wise do not cross every river alone.

You do not need to become calm today. You only need to stop stirring. Stillness will find you. It always does, when we stop chasing it.

Softly,
Lao Tzu
