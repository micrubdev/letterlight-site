Envy is uncomfortable, and most of us feel ashamed of it. But it carries useful information. It often points at something you want and haven't let yourself go after. These prompts help you look at the feeling without judging yourself, and use what it shows you.

## How to use these prompts

- Pick one prompt and write for five to ten minutes.
- Write honestly. No one else will read this, so you don't need to sound generous.
- If a prompt brings up a specific person, that's fine. Keep the focus on what you can learn about yourself.
- Come back to the last section when you are ready to act.

## Noticing the feeling

- When did you last feel a sting of envy? What were you looking at or hearing?
- Where do you feel envy in your body? Describe it.
- Which places, apps or conversations leave you feeling worse about your own life?
- Who do you compare yourself to most often? Do you compare yourself to them in one area or many?

## What is underneath it

- Finish this sentence five times: "I envy them because they have..."
- Look at your list. Which item do you actually want, and which do you only want to be seen having?
- Is the feeling more about what they have, or about what you fear you lack?
- What story do you tell yourself about why they got it and you didn't? How much of it is true?

## Seeing the full picture

- What might that person have given up, or struggled with, that you don't see?
- What are you comparing: their highlights, or their ordinary days?
- What do you have that someone else might envy? List at least five things.
- Who in your life is quietly glad of what you have? How does that feel to notice?

## Turning it into a goal

- Pick one thing you envy. What would it look like to work toward a version of it that suits your life?
- What is the smallest step you could take toward it this week?
- What skill or habit does the person you envy practise? How could you begin practising it?
- If you stopped comparing for one month, what would you do with the time and energy?

## Letting it go

- Write a short note wishing the person you envy well. You don't have to mean it fully yet.
- What would change if their success had nothing to do with your worth?
- What are you proud of this year, measured only against who you were last year?
- Write down one thing you are grateful for today that has nothing to do with anyone else.
