Starting over can follow a breakup, a move, a lost job, or a slow realisation that the old life no longer fits. It usually brings a mix of grief, relief and fear, sometimes all in one afternoon. These prompts help you sort through what ended and work out, one small step at a time, what comes next.

## How to use these prompts

- Pick one prompt per sitting. Ten minutes is plenty.
- Write by hand if you can. It slows your thoughts down.
- There are no wrong answers. If a prompt doesn't fit your situation, skip it.
- Reread your entries after a few weeks. You'll likely see how far you've come.

## Looking back

- What ended, and how did it end? Write the story in plain words.
- What are you grieving, even if part of you is glad it's over?
- What did the old life give you that you want to carry forward?
- What did it cost you that you don't want to pay again?

## Where you are now

- Describe an ordinary day in your life right now, honestly.
- What feels hardest about this in-between stage?
- What, if anything, feels lighter or freer than before?
- Who has been there for you during this change? Who could you lean on more?

## What you want

- If you could design the next year from scratch, what would it include?
- What did you love doing before the old life took up all your time?
- What do you want more of, and less of, in the next chapter?
- Whose expectations are you ready to stop living by?
- What would you try if you weren't afraid of looking foolish?

## First steps

- What is one small thing you could do this week that your new life would include?
- What skill, contact or information do you need first? How could you get it?
- What routine would give your days some shape while everything else is uncertain?
- What could you clear out, physically or otherwise, to make room?

## Keeping going

- Write down one thing you did this week that took courage, however small.
- When you feel like going back to the old life, what will you remind yourself?
- Write a letter from yourself a year from now, describing what changed.
- What do you want to say to yourself on the hard days? Write it so you can find it again.
