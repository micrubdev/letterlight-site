A gratitude list can turn stale fast. Write "my family, my health, coffee" every day for a month and the words stop meaning anything. These prompts push you toward the specific: a named person, a particular afternoon, a small thing you'd stopped noticing.

## How to use these prompts

- Pick one prompt a day. Specific beats long: one detailed paragraph is better than ten general lines.
- Write it in the evening, or first thing in the morning before your phone.
- On bad days, it's fine to write "I don't feel grateful today" and then answer the smallest prompt you can find.
- Gratitude isn't a way to argue yourself out of real problems. You can be thankful for something and still struggle.

## Small things

- What is one thing that went right today that you'd normally overlook?
- Describe a meal you enjoyed recently. Who made it? What did it taste like?
- What object in your home do you use every day and would miss if it vanished?
- What sound, smell or view made you pause this week?
- Which part of your body worked well for you today, and what did it let you do?
- What's a small comfort you can always come back to?

## People

- Who made your life a little easier this week, even if they didn't know it?
- Write about a teacher or mentor who changed the way you think.
- Who do you trust completely? What did they do to earn that trust?
- Describe a stranger's kindness you still remember.
- Who is someone you've never thanked properly? What would you say to them?
- Which friend can you call when things go wrong? What do they do that helps?

## Looking back

- What is something you have now that you once wished for?
- Think of a hard time you came through. What or who helped you?
- What's a mistake you're grateful you made, because of what it taught you?
- What skill do you have now that took you years to learn?
- Which place from your past are you glad you got to live in or visit?
- What did you learn from someone you didn't get along with?

## Hard days

- What is still okay today, even though a lot isn't?
- What is one thing you're looking forward to, however small?
- Who would you want beside you right now? Write about why.
- What did you manage to do today, even if it was only getting through it?
- What part of your situation could be worse, and what is keeping it from being worse?
- Write about a time you felt this way before and it passed.

## Going deeper

- What do you take for granted that someone else would give a lot to have?
- What about your daily life would your ten-years-younger self be amazed by?
- How could you show your gratitude to someone in a way they'd actually notice?
- What is something about yourself you're grateful for?
- What's a book, song or film that came into your life at exactly the right time?
- If you could thank one person from history for something you have today, who would it be?
