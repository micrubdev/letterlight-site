Burnout makes everything feel like one more task, and that includes journaling. So keep this light. These prompts are here to help you see what is wearing you down and what you might change, one small piece at a time.

## How to use these prompts

- Pick one prompt. That's enough for today.
- Write for five minutes, or until you run out of things to say, whichever comes first.
- Don't try to solve everything in one entry. Noticing is already progress.
- If a prompt makes you feel worse, skip it and come back another week.

## Noticing where you are

- On a scale of one to ten, how tired are you today? What would move that number one point lower?
- When did you last feel rested after a weekend? What was different then?
- What does your body do when you think about work: tight chest, clenched jaw, a heavy stomach? Describe it.
- Which part of your day do you dread most, and which part do you look forward to, even a little?
- What have you stopped doing for fun because you felt too tired?

## What is draining you

- List everything you are responsible for right now. Circle the items no one else could do.
- Which task takes the most energy for the least reward?
- Whose expectations are you trying to meet? Are they ones you agree with?
- What do you keep saying yes to that you wish you could refuse?
- Is it the amount of work that wears you out, or the kind of work? Write about the difference.
- Where are you giving more than you get back, at work or at home?

## Letting things go

- Name one obligation you could drop or hand to someone else this month. What is stopping you?
- What would "good enough" look like for the task you are stuck on?
- Write the message you would send to set a boundary. You don't have to send it yet.
- What would you tell a friend who described your week to you?

## Finding your way back

- Describe a day that would feel restful. What is one piece of it you could have this weekend?
- What made you choose this work in the first place? Is any of that still there?
- Who leaves you feeling lighter after you see them? When can you see them next?
- What is one small thing that gives you energy instead of taking it?
- If nothing had to change for a year except one thing, what would you choose?
- What do you need to hear right now? Write it to yourself.
- What would your life look like if rest were a priority rather than a reward?
