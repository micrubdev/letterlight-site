Sometimes the thing on your mind is a document: a review, a letter, an article you can't stop thinking about. You can attach it instead of describing it.

Sources are stored on your phone and sent with the entry when you ask for a letter or a reply, so leave out anything you would not want to send. Remove one by tapping its cross. [How your data is handled](/privacy.html).
