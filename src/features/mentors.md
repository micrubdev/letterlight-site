Who you write to changes what comes back. A Stoic may ask what is in your control, while a poet may ask what you noticed.

Mentors are AI personas inspired by these figures. One of them is nobody famous: Future You, the person you are becoming.

You can read [sample letters from 15 of them](/letters/) on this site.
