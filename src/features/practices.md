A letter can be good company and still leave you with nothing to do tomorrow. So a mentor may add one or two exercises, each meant to take under ten minutes.

They draw on methods from cognitive behavioural therapy, or CBT: writing down a thought and one fact against it, taking one small step, slow breathing, checking your mood. The app is not therapy, does not replace it, and cannot help in an emergency.
