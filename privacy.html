Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Letterlight is a journal app for Android. This page explains what happens to what you write.

What stays on your phone

  • Your journal entries, moods, letters, replies, routine and practices are saved only in the app's storage on your device.
  • Files you attach as sources (PDFs, images, text) are copied into the app's storage on your device.
  • Any API key you enter, and the address and model of another provider if you set one up, are saved on your device.
  • A usage log (how many tokens each request used, and what it was for) is kept on your device so you can see what letters cost.
  • Your invite code, and the device token Letterlight's server gives you for it, are saved on your device. There is no Letterlight account.

What is sent to the AI model

Letters are written by an AI model. By default this is Claude, made by Anthropic. When you ask for a letter, a reply or a translation, the app sends these to the model's provider:

  • the entry and mood you're asking about, and the earlier letters and replies in that conversation;
  • any sources attached to that entry;
  • the mentor you chose, your app language and the local date and time;
  • for routine and practice reminders, the habit or exercise they are about.

Anthropic processes this under its own terms and privacy policy.

Letterlight's server

By default, these requests go through Letterlight's server, which runs on Cloudflare. It passes each request to Anthropic, using Letterlight's own Anthropic account, and returns the letter to your phone. This is why you don't need an API key of your own.

  • The server doesn't store your entries, letters, replies or sources, and doesn't write them to its logs.
  • To keep to daily limits and a budget, it keeps counts: a random device number given to your phone (shown in logs only in a shortened, scrambled form), the kind of request, how many tokens it used and what it cost.
  • Cloudflare carries the traffic under its own privacy policy.

If you choose "My Anthropic key" in Settings, requests go from your phone straight to Anthropic under your own account, and Letterlight's server isn't involved.

If you choose another provider

In Settings you can connect a different AI service instead: any server that speaks the OpenAI Chat Completions API, such as OpenAI or a model you run yourself. You enter its address, model and key. The app then sends the same information listed above to that address instead of to Anthropic, under your account with that service. That service's own terms and privacy policy apply. Letterlight doesn't choose, check or have access to it.

Web pages you add

When you add a web page as a source, the app downloads that page directly from its website once and keeps its text on your device.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking libraries.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • Notifications are scheduled on your phone; no push server is involved.

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app, or uninstall Letterlight to remove everything it stored on your device. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings does the same.

Children

Letterlight is not directed at children under 13.
